# World Map - Midgaard

## Central Areas

### Temple Of Midgaard (Starting Location)
- Description: Giant marble blocks, ancient wall paintings with Gods and giants
- Exits: n (up), e (east to reading room/donation), s (south to temple square), w, d (down)
- NPCs: Automatic teller machine
- Items: None noted

### Temple Square
- Description: Huge marble steps leading to temple gate
- Exits: n (temple), e (grunting boar inn), s (market square), w (clerics guild area)
- NPCs: None noted
- Features: Large fountain (blue-streaked marble)

### Market Square
- Description: Famous Square of Midgaard, large peculiar statue in middle
- Exits: n (temple square), s (common square), e, w (main street)
- NPCs/Items: Oozing green gelatinous blob
- Notes: Center of Midgaard, roads lead in every direction

### Inn Areas
- **Grunting Boar Inn Entrance Hall**: Post office to north, bar to east
  - Exits: n (post office), e (bar), w (temple square), u (reception)
- **The Grunting Boar (Bar)**: Bar counter, fireplace, windows to southeast
  - NPCs: City guard, 3 singing drunk patrons, bartender
  - Exits: w (entrance hall)

### Poor Alley District
- **Eastern End Of Poor Alley**: Leads to common square east, alley continues west
  - Exits: e (common square), s (grubby inn), w (alley continues)
  - NPCs: 3 beastly fidos (garbage dogs)
- **Poor Alley (Western section)**: City wall to west
  - Exits: e (eastern end), w (city wall)
  - NPCs: Beggar

### Grubby Inn
- Description: Old, dirty, vile smells, not cleaned for decades
- Exits: n (east end of poor alley)
- NPCs: Beggar, Filthy (bartender)

## Wilderness/Outer Areas

### Inn Complex (Above Recommended Level)
- **Front Of The Inn**: Wooden building with smoke, painted sign visible
  - Exits: n (north side), e (behind), s (south side), w (west/garbage dump)
- **North Of The Inn**: Smells of smoke and cooking
  - Exits: s (front)
  - Note: Only one exit, dead end with cooking smells
- **Behind The Inn**: Garbage area
  - Exits: e (back around), w (garbage dump)
- **South Of The Inn**: Trail with muffled cries
  - Exits: n (front), s (ambush point), w (rear/garbage)

### Forest/Mountain Areas (Above Recommended Level)
- **Trail To Miden'Nir**: Light forest, smoke rises to west, Midgaard to north
  - Exits: n (midgaard), e (miden'nir), s, w
- **The Miden'nir / Miden'nir**: Dark forest, mountains to east
  - NPCs: Mountain goblins (2+)
  - Exits: e (mountains block), s, w
- **The Ambush Point**: Overgrown trail, battle damage visible
  - Exits: n (south of inn), w (trail continues)
  - NPCs: Small boy (wounded)
- **The Deep Forest**: Dark, foreboding
  - Exits: n, e, s, w
  - NPCs: Mountain goblin

### Other Areas
- **On The Concourse**: Southwest corner near city wall
  - Exits: n (toward shops), e, w
- **Wall Road**: Western city wall, bridge to south
  - Exits: n (north), s (bridge/river)
- **Muddy Ground**: Southwest area

- **Near The Mountains**: Rocky area, mountains to east
